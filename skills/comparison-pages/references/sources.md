# Sources (checked 2026-11-12)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Spam policies: scaled content abuse (many pages mainly to manipulate rankings, unoriginal content); thin affiliate pages (reusing merchant descriptions and reviews without original value; stronger pages add original testing, price information, comparisons); link spam (paid links acceptable if marked `nofollow` or `sponsored`); misleading functionality (a claimed comparison calculator that does not work) | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies |
| Review snippet guidelines: self-serving reviews make LocalBusiness or Organization pages ineligible for stars (including third-party widgets); reviews must refer to a specific item, not a category or list; ratings and review text must be visible; ratings from users; do not aggregate reviews from other websites; no fake or undisclosed incentivized reviews | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet |
| FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials, effective 2024-10-21: prohibits fake or false reviews and testimonials (16 CFR 465.2), buying reviews with required sentiment (465.4), insider reviews without clear disclosure (465.5), misrepresenting company-controlled sites as independent (465.6), review suppression by intimidation or unfounded legal threats (465.7), fake social media indicators (465.8); civil penalties for knowing violations (the page states no dollar amount) | https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers |
| Helpful content: original information, added value over other results, sourcing and author background, AI disclosure | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content |
| SoftwareApplication requires aggregateRating or review; violations can lead to manual action | https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/software-app |
| Before/after measurement | `skills/measure-rollback/references/method.md` |

## Not verified
- FTC civil penalty amount per violation: the FTC Q&A page read gives none, so the skill states none.
- Whether the FTC rule applies to a given comparison page: depends on facts and jurisdiction. The skill tells the user to check the FTC page and consult a lawyer, and gives no legal conclusion.
- Google's wording on "self-serving" covers LocalBusiness and Organization pages; Google's page does not name SaaS comparison pages. The no-review-markup rule for comparison pages rests on the "specific item, not a list" requirement, and on caution.
