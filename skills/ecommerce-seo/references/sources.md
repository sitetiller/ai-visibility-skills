## Tool behaviour (sitetiller 0.1.0, 2026-11-12)
- `robots-test` on a three-line draft robots.txt: `/shoes?color=red` returned BLOCKED with rule `Disallow: /*?color= (line 2)`; `/shoes` and `/products/blue-mug` returned allowed. (Git Bash on Windows rewrote the leading slash of two paths in the printed table; this is a shell artifact, not a tool result.)
- `cli/lib/schema-lint.mjs`: Product requires `name` and one of `review`, `aggregateRating`, `offers`; recommends `image`, `brand`, `offers.price`. No merchant listing checks (no `priceCurrency`, price above zero, shipping or returns).
- `cli/bin/sitetiller.mjs`: `robots-test <robots.txt> <path...> [--agents A,B]`.
